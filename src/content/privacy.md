## 4. Data Security and Retention

- **Security:** Credentials and other secrets are encrypted in transit using TLS, and stored secrets are encrypted at rest. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized {{APP_NAME}} personnel who need it for support, billing, or audit purposes.
- **Retention:** We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes above. In practice:
  - Merchant transaction and ledger records are retained while your account is active and for the period required by applicable tax and record-keeping law.
  - Recipient delivery data is retained for the operational life of the order plus the period required to resolve disputes.
  - Rider identity documents and photographs are retained while the rider's account remains active, and deleted within a reasonable period of account closure unless a legal obligation requires longer.
- **Deletion:** On account closure we delete or de-identify personal data not subject to a retention obligation, once all outstanding balances and fees are settled.
- **Breaches:** If a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority — {{PRIVACY_AUTHORITY}} — and, where required, notify affected individuals without undue delay.
