## 1. Data We Collect

- **Merchant Corporate Data:** Full name, registered business name, commercial Trade License number, Tax Identification Number (TIN), National ID (NID) copy, physical pickup warehouse address, email address, corporate telephone lines, and verified banking/MFS (bKash and Nagad) account information.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data strictly for lawful purposes under operational and regulatory necessity:

- Fulfilling delivery mandates, dispatching automated SMS tracking alerts, and verifying deliveries.
- Processing financial disbursements, deducting service fees, and routing COD earnings to your configured bKash or Nagad accounts.
- Mitigating e-commerce delivery fraud, preventing the shipment of illegal items, and managing insurance or damage claims under the BDT 1,00,000 parcel value cap.
- Complying with statutory reporting requirements issued by the Bangladesh Bank and national law enforcement bodies.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

{{APP_NAME}} does not rent, sell, or trade personal data to third-party marketing brokers. Data sharing is limited to the following interactions:

- **Operational Partners:** We share customer names, addresses, and phone numbers with our authorized field Delivery Agents and local line-haul contractors to execute physical delivery.
- **MFS & Banking Gateways:** Merchant settlement data is securely shared with our digital payment partners (bKash and Nagad) exclusively to authorize automated next-day financial payouts.
- **Legal Mandates:** We will disclose data to law enforcement agencies, regular courts, or the Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission (BTRC) if required by law or a valid court subpoena.

- **Security Infrastructure:** All merchant credentials, financial accounts, and consumer records are encrypted in transit using Secure Socket Layer (SSL/TLS) mechanisms and stored on secure cloud environments protected by advanced corporate firewalls.

- **Retention Timelines:** We retain business transactions and log history for as long as your merchant account remains active, or for a period required to fulfill long-term tax audit liabilities and central bank compliance laws in Bangladesh.
